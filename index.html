The "No" Philosophy

Modern apps want your data, your attention, and your monthly subscription. PennyPilot takes a different approach.

  • ✕
    No Cloud Sync Your data stays on your device. Period.
  • ✕
    No Bank Connections We don't want your credentials. Manual entry ensures mindfulness.
  • ✕
    No Ads or Tracking You are the user, not the product.
  • ✓
    Yes to Freedom Export your data as CSV/JSON anytime. You own it.
